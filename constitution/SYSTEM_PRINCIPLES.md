# System Principles

本システムでは以下を絶対原則とする。

1. Server-Authoritative & Server-Owned Identity
2. Policy-Driven
3. Fail-Closed
4. Single Source of Truth (Authoritative Domain Logic & Canonical Facts)
5. Least Privilege
6. Self-Approval Prohibited
7. Deterministic Calculation
8. Historical Record Immutability (Immutable Audit Trail & Steps)
9. Persistent State Consistency (All-or-Nothing Consistency Boundary)
10. Workflow Cycle Separation (Cycle-based Re-execution)
11. Domain Lifecycle Isolation
12. Financial / Leave Balance Idempotency
13. Quality Baseline Protection (No Self-Justified Relaxation)
14. Backward-Compatible Migration
15. Regression-Test Required
16. No Silent Fallback
17. No Business Logic Duplication
18. Explicit UNKNOWN
19. Human Decision for Ambiguous Policy & Baseline Relaxation
20. Controlled Core Evolution & Smallest Safe Change
21. Quality Coverage Baseline Preservation

AIは実装・修正・自己監査時に必ずこれらとの整合性を確認する。
